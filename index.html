MTB academy curriculum

Master the trail.
One skill at a time.

A practical progression from first fundamentals to technical trail riding. Start easy, practise with control, then level up when each skill feels natural.

01

Ready position

Build a stable stance with bent elbows and knees, level pedals, relaxed hands and eyes looking ahead.

02

Braking

Learn smooth braking with both brakes, control weight transfer and slow down before steep or technical sections.

03

Cornering

Brake before the turn, look through the exit, lean the bike and keep traction through the whole corner.

04

Climbing

Choose the right gear, keep a steady cadence and find the sweet spot between seated traction and body position.

05

Descending

Stay centred, keep your vision up and use progressive braking while the bike moves underneath you.

06

Rocks & roots

Relax your grip, pick a clean line and let the bike roll over rough ground without fighting every movement.

07

Jumps

Start small and learn take-off, body position and controlled landings on a safe, suitable feature.

08

Bike setup

Understand tyre pressure, suspension, saddle position and cockpit setup so your bike works with you.

09

Safety

Ride within your ability, wear appropriate protection, carry essentials and know when to walk a feature.

10

Trail etiquette

Respect other riders, walkers, wildlife and trail rules. Ride predictably, control speed and leave no trace.

11

Repairs & maintenance

Learn essential checks and fixes including punctures, chains, brakes, bolts and pre-ride inspections.

12

Progression plan

Turn practice into progress with simple goals: control first, consistency second, speed and difficulty last.

Trail playbook

Tips for the
tricky bits.

Climbing

Find traction

Use a sustainable gear, keep a steady cadence, sit centred, relax your upper body and shift your weight only as much as needed to stop the rear wheel from spinning.

Trick: On loose climbs, smooth pedalling usually beats brute force.

Descending

Stay centred

Look well down the trail, keep elbows and knees bent, brake before obstacles and use both brakes progressively. Move your hips back on steep sections without hanging off the rear.

Trick: Slow down before the difficult feature, not halfway through it.

Safety & etiquette

Ride prepared.
Respect the trail.

⛑

Protect yourself

Wear a properly fitted helmet. For technical riding, consider eye protection and appropriate gloves and pads. Carry water, a phone, basic tools and a repair kit.

✓

Know your limits

Walk a feature if you are unsure. Build difficulty gradually, ride with a buddy when possible, and tell someone your route on longer or remote rides.

↺

Share the trail

Follow local signs and access rules, give way appropriately, control your speed and avoid damaging wet or fragile trails. Leave no litter behind.

Bike setup

Small adjustments.
Big difference.

Before a ride, check tyre pressure for the terrain and rider weight, test both brakes, confirm wheels and axles are secure, inspect the drivetrain and suspension, and make sure your contact points feel comfortable.

Tyres Check pressure, tread and damage
Brakes Firm levers, healthy pads and rotors
Controls Secure cockpit and comfortable position
